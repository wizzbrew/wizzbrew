# Lower Norfair (`norfair_lower`): the Pirates' deepest holdout, Golden Torizo and Ridley

- **Look and mood:** a dark, ruined, smoldering underworld; the Pirates' deepest holdout.
- **Lives there:** Ridley, the Golden Torizo, Fire Fleas, Space Pirates, and a room players call the Worst Room (VARIA presets use the name).
- **Vanilla items:** Screw Attack, Missile and Super Missile (Golden Torizo's room), Energy Tank (Ridley), Power Bombs of shame.
- **Remember:** Varia is needed to enter; Ridley's lair is at the bottom.

## Entry rule

The randomizer splits Lower Norfair into two halves (regions). Both need Varia and the Upper Norfair East entry and PB (Morph Ball and a Power Bomb pack).

- **West:** C: plus Space Jump and Gravity. T: plus (Hi-Jump or Gravity).
- **East:** C: same as Casual West. T: West's rule plus (FLY or Hi-Jump or SBJ or (Ice and Charge)) and (PASS or (Screw Attack and Space Jump)) and (Morph or TANKS(5)).

## Bosses and minibosses

- **Golden Torizo.** A gilded Chozo statue come to life, 13,500 HP, a golden version of the Bomb Torizo. He catches Super Missiles you fire and throws them back; Super Missiles do 600 damage to him instead of 300. Holds a Missile and a Super Missile pack in his room. Remembered as the hard optional boss; speedruns often skip him (skip Golden Torizo unless you need his items). In logic he is not modeled as a fight; his two ammo items are rules on Lower Norfair West: Missile (Gold Torizo) C: PB, Space Jump, Super. T: PB, Space Jump, Varia and (Hi-Jump or Gravity). Super Missile (Gold Torizo) C: DBW, (Super or Charge), Space Jump, PB. T: DBW, Varia and (Super or Charge).
- **Ridley.** The Space Pirate commander, a winged dragon; he also appears at Ceres, where you must hit him quickly or he flees. 18,000 HP in his lair; he enrages at 50, 30 and 10 percent health and his tail is invulnerable. His room holds Energy Tank (Ridley), and his death counts toward the Golden Four. Remembered as the series' signature villain. Golden Four member. In logic he is not modeled: Energy Tank (Ridley) in Lower Norfair East needs PB and Super on top of the region entry.

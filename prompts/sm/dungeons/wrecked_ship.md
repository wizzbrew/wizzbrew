# Wrecked Ship (`wrecked_ship`): the crashed ship and Phantoon

- **Look and mood:** a crashed, dead ship with flickering lights and ghostly enemies; eerie, with a haunted-house feel.
- **Lives there:** Phantoon, work robots, ghosts called Coverns, and Skultera fish in the flooded decks.
- **Vanilla items:** Gravity Suit, a Reserve Tank, an Energy Tank, Missile and Super Missile packs.
- **Remember:** the ship has "power", and the lights and robots come alive after Phantoon dies.

## Entry rule

C: Super Missile and PB (Morph Ball and a Power Bomb pack) and WSX, where WSX is Space Jump or Speed Booster or Grapple or (Gravity and (FLY or Hi-Jump)). T: Super Missile and PB. The ship sits in the moat off Crateria east, which itself needs PB and Super.

## Bosses and minibosses

- **Phantoon.** A ghost, 2500 HP, that flies in figure-8s and drops flames; only his open eye takes damage. He turns invisible when he closes his eye, and rains flames from the ceiling until you hit him. Defeating him powers the ship. Remembered for the invisibility and for "Sunrise Surprise" (several flames at once). Golden Four member. In logic he is not modeled: the Wrecked Ship is entered with Super and PB (Casual adds WSX), and the location rules say nothing about Phantoon.

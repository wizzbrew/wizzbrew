# Crateria (`crateria`): the surface

- **Look and mood:** rainy, stormy surface around the gunship, with old Chozo and Pirate ruins; foreboding and quiet.
- **Lives there:** Space Pirates, Geemers, Skree, and the Wrecked Ship sitting off to the east in the moat.
- **Vanilla items:** Bombs (from the Bomb Torizo statue), Energy Tanks at Terminator and the Gauntlet, plus Missile, Super Missile and Power Bomb packs.
- **Remember:** the Golden Four statue room is here, and Crateria connects to every other area. The first Tourian was folded into Crateria as ruins.

## Entry rule

The randomizer splits Crateria into three parts (the code calls them regions):

- **Central:** always open.
- **West:** destroy bomb walls (DBW: Morph Ball and Bombs or a Power Bomb pack, or Screw Attack) or Speed Booster.
- **East (the Wrecked Ship moat side):** Morph Ball with a Power Bomb pack (PB) and Super Missile, in both logics.

## Bosses and minibosses

- **Bomb Torizo.** An ancient Chozo statue that holds Bombs in an item sphere; when you take them, it wakes up and fights. It throws explosive orbs that look like item spheres; shoot them for health and ammo. The Bombs are the prize. Remembered as often the first boss players face, and the moment a quiet statue turns hostile. Not part of the Golden Four. In logic the Bombs item is the location Bombs (Bomb Torizo): C: PASS and RED. T: Morph and RED. No kill rule is modeled.
- **The Golden Four (G4).** Four statues in a Crateria room, one each for Kraid (green eyes), Phantoon (yellow), Draygon (blue) and Ridley (red). Each boss kill breaks that statue's eye and turns it grey; when all four are grey the statues sink, opening the elevator to Tourian. "G4" is the shorthand (VARIA's option is "Golden 4"). The randomizer ROM opens the Tourian path from the corridor before the statues once the four boss-defeated flags are set; by the RAM layout these are Kraid, Phantoon, Draygon and Ridley. Mother Brain cutscenes are shortened.

# Maridia (`maridia`): the flooded caves, Botwoon and Draygon

- **Look and mood:** a slow underwater, sand and glass-tube world with moody music; often called the most atmospheric area, and the one many players dread.
- **Lives there:** Mochtroids, Puyos, Snails, Mama Turtle, Botwoon (miniboss), Draygon (boss).
- **Vanilla items:** Spring Ball (Pants Room), Plasma Beam, Space Jump, Reserve Tank, Energy Tanks, Super Missiles.
- **Remember:** without Gravity Suit you move slowly in water. Suitless Maridia is a Tournament-logic option.

## Entry rule

The randomizer splits Maridia into two parts (regions). Both need the Upper Norfair West entry and PB (Morph Ball and a Power Bomb pack).

- **Outer:** C: Gravity. T: Gravity or (Hi-Jump and (SBJ or Ice)).
- **Inner:** C: Gravity and (FLY or Speed or Grapple). T: Gravity or (Hi-Jump and (Ice or SBJ) and Grapple). So suitless Maridia is Tournament only.

## Bosses and minibosses

- **Botwoon.** A serpent, 3000 HP, that snakes around the room and spits acid; only his head takes damage. Killing him gives Energy Tank (Botwoon) and leads on toward Draygon (the logic gates Draygon's rewards behind him). Remembered for a bug where leaving early leaves him in the room. In logic, BOT (CanDefeatBotwoon: C: Speed Booster. T: Ice, or Speed and Gravity) gates Missile (Draygon) (T also needs Gravity), Energy Tank (Botwoon) (also needs Gravity or Ice), and everything behind DRAY.
- **Draygon.** A huge sea creature, 6000 HP, that swoops and sprays mucus; she can grab you in a long spiral. The speedrun wiki calls her the toughest fight to execute properly. The Grapple trick: while she holds you, fire Grapple at an exposed wall turret; the electricity kills her in about 4 seconds. Defeating her opens the way to Space Jump. Remembered for that kill. Golden Four member. In logic, DRAY (CanDefeatDraygon) is the only explicit boss rule: it gates Plasma Beam and Space Jump. C: Speed, Gravity and (Hi-Jump or FLY). T: Gravity plus BOT (Ice, or Speed and Gravity), so effectively Gravity and (Ice or Speed). The corpse trivia: if Draygon's corpse drifts out of bounds on the left, it loops back after about 18 minutes and 10 seconds.

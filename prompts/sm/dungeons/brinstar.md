# Brinstar (`brinstar`): green, pink and red zones, and Kraid's lair

- **Look and mood:** a lush, overgrown jungle first, then pink and red soil caverns; the Red Brinstar track is among the best known.
- **Lives there:** Zoomers, Rippers, Reos, Sidehoppers, and the Etecoons and Dachora (the friendly animals).
- **Vanilla items:** Morphing Ball, Charge Beam (Pink Brinstar), Spazer, X-Ray Scope, Varia Suit (past Kraid), a Reserve Tank, Energy Tanks, Super Missiles and Power Bombs.
- **Remember:** new players spend their first hour here; the Early Supers route uses the mockball. The Red Tower is the vertical shaft into Red Brinstar.

## Entry rule

The randomizer splits Brinstar into five parts (regions):

- **Blue:** always open.
- **Green:** destroy bomb walls (DBW: Morph Ball and Bombs or a Power Bomb pack, or Screw Attack) or Speed Booster.
- **Pink:** (open red doors, RED, and (DBW or Speed)) or PB (Morph Ball and a Power Bomb pack).
- **Red:** BASE, which is (DBW or Speed) and Super Missile and Morph Ball.
- **Kraid's lair:** (DBW or Speed) and Super Missile and PASS (Morph Ball with Bombs or a Power Bomb pack).

## Bosses and minibosses

- **Spore Spawn.** A giant, plant-like pod, 960 HP, that hangs from the ceiling. It drifts in a figure-8 dropping spores, then opens its core after about 13 seconds. Beyond it lies the way to the first Super Missiles (the speedrun wiki links it to the Brinstar Super Missile list). Remembered as an early boss that needs a plan. Not modeled in logic: Pink Brinstar entry and its location rules stand alone.
- **Kraid** (Kraid's lair). A huge reptilian creature allied with the Pirates, 1000 HP. He rises from the floor, throws claws and stones, and is damageable only through the mouth (hit the eye to open it). Killing him opens the Varia Suit room. Remembered for his sheer size. Golden Four member. In logic: region "Kraid's lair" needs (DBW or Speed) and Super and PASS. Inside it Varia Suit and Energy Tank (Kraid) have no further rule; Missile (Kraid) needs PB. The kill requirement is not stated.

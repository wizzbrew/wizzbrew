# Tourian (`tourian`): the Pirate control center and Mother Brain

- **Look and mood:** a metallic, mechanical lair; fast, pounding music; no items to collect.
- **Lives there:** Metroids in glass cases, Rinkas, Zebetites, Mother Brain.
- **Vanilla items:** none (the Hyper Beam comes from the baby Metroid).
- **Remember:** Metroids are freezing-then-missile targets.

## Entry rule

Tourian is not modeled in the logic: it has no item locations and no entry rule. The randomizer ROM patch opens the path to it from the corridor before the Golden Four statues once the four boss-defeated flags are set, which by the RAM layout are Kraid, Phantoon, Draygon and Ridley. In the unrandomized game the same four kills sink the statues and open the elevator to Tourian. Mother Brain is the goal and is not part of the logic; her cutscenes are shortened.

## Bosses and minibosses

- **Mother Brain.** A giant bio-computer in a glass tank, three forms: the tank (3000 HP), her standing body (18,000 HP) and the Hyper Beam finish (36,000 HP, 36 shots of 1000). The baby Metroid drains her and then dies; the Hyper Beam comes from the baby. Remembered for the baby's return and sacrifice. In speedrunning the question is Murder Beam versus charged Ice, Wave and Plasma on Mother Brain.

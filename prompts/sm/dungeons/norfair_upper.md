# Upper Norfair (`norfair_upper`): the hot red caves and Crocomire

- **Look and mood:** hot, red-lit caves, lava, heat that drains your energy without Varia; tense and driving.
- **Lives there:** Multiviolas, Geruta, Holtz, and Crocomire as the miniboss.
- **Vanilla items:** Ice Beam, Hi-Jump Boots, Speed Booster, Wave Beam, Grappling Beam (beyond Crocomire), a Reserve Tank.
- **Remember:** bubble Norfair (Speed Booster side) and the long hell run corridors.

## Entry rule

The randomizer splits Upper Norfair into three parts (regions). BASE is (destroy bomb walls or Speed Booster) and Super Missile and Morph Ball. HELL is Varia or 5 tanks.

- **West** (Ice Beam and Hi-Jump side, no heat rule at entry): BASE.
- **East** (bubble Norfair, Speed Booster, Wave): C: BASE and Varia. T: BASE and HELL (the extra "Super or (Spd and PB)" is redundant since BASE already holds Super).
- **Crocomire:** C: BASE and Varia and (PB and Speed, or Speed and Wave, or Morph and (FLY or Hi-Jump) and Wave). T: BASE and (TANKS(2) and Speed, or HELL) and (FLY or Hi-Jump or SBJ or (Varia and Ice) or Speed) and (PASS or Speed or (Varia and Morph)).

## Bosses and minibosses

- **Crocomire.** A beast with no health bar: hits push him back, and the goal is to push him into the acid in the back of the room. Spikes can catch you. Killing him leads to the Grappling Beam and an Energy Tank. Remembered for his death: he melts away, leaving a skeleton. In speedruns he is saved until after Plasma. In logic he is not modeled as a kill: his region is the Crocomire entry rule above, and the items near him have their own rules.

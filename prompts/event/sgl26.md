# SpeedGaming Live 2026: event facts

SpeedGaming Live 2026 (SGL26) is run by SpeedGaming. It has tournaments played on site and tournaments played online.

## Dates and venue

- On site: Thursday October 22 to Sunday October 25, 2026.
- Venue: DoubleTree by Hilton Washington Dulles Airport, 13869 Park Center Road, Herndon, VA 20171. Phone +1 703-478-2900.
- The online tournaments ran from summer to early October 2026 and have finished.

## Hotel

- Group block booking link: https://www.hilton.com/en/attend-my-event/iaddddt-sgt-dbdc1c4d-2085-440e-abcd-94c590cebeee/
- Rate: $129 per night, 2 Doubles or 1 King, with free breakfast.
- Recommended arrival is Wednesday October 21 and departure is Monday October 26.
- The Hilton form opens with different default dates, so adjust the dates in the form to match.

## Tickets

- Everyone on site needs a ticket, players and spectators alike. The online tournaments need no ticket.
- Daily, $40: the event spaces and the community tournaments for one day.
- Base, $80: all spaces and tournaments, plus breakfast.
- VIP, $110: Base plus a supporter badge, lanyard, shiny pin and your name in the stream thank-you.
- SUPER VIP, $160: VIP plus a shiny T-shirt.
- Tickets are on Matcherino: https://matcherino.com/t/sglive26
- To add to the prize pool, contribute at https://matcherino.com/t/sglive26?contribute=true and name the game it supports in the contribution comment.

# Level 2, the Moon (`level2`)

- **Shape:** Moon.
- **Boss:** Dodongo, a rhino that eats bombs and dies.
- **Vanilla items:** Magical Boomerang, plus the Heart Container, Triforce piece, map and compass.
- **Vanilla access:** no item gate is documented. Z1R states that the first quest lakes at Levels 2 and 7 always open with the Recorder.
- **Boss requirement:** bombs fed to it; a sword also works with enough hits.
- **Hint phrases:** Dodongo Dwells. The line DODONGO DISLIKES SMOKE says bombs kill it.

## Remember

Dodongo is Level 2 in the first quest and Level 3 in the second. The wiki's example of a dungeon-only cave shuffle has Level 2 needing the Raft and Level 4 needing the Recorder.

# Level 1, the Eagle (`level1`)

- **Shape:** Eagle, named for what its map looks like in the first quest.
- **Boss:** Aquamentus, a horned dragon that fires triple fireballs. It is also the boss of Level 7.
- **Vanilla items:** Bow and Boomerang, plus the Heart Container, Triforce piece, map and compass that every level from 1 to 8 holds.
- **Vanilla access:** no item gate. It is the easiest level and the usual first stop.
- **Boss requirement:** any weapon; hit the horn with the sword or its beam.
- **Hint phrases:** Aquamentus Awaits. The line EASTMOST PENNINSULA IS THE SECRET is a Level 1 hint; a cave in the northeast holds 100 rupees.

## Remember

Candle Row is the cluster of burn bushes by Level 1. With cave shuffle the entrance can sit behind any gate, and the Dungeon Quest setting can swap in a second quest layout for it. The Bow and Boomerang are the vanilla items here, but Z1R can put any item on any floor.

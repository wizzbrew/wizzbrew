# Level 3, the Manji (`level3`)

- **Shape:** Manji. Thonky spells it Manji and the Z1R wiki writes Kanji; Manji is the right one.
- **Boss:** Manhandla, a multi-mouthed plant that speeds up as its mouths die.
- **Vanilla items:** Raft, plus the Heart Container, Triforce piece, map and compass.
- **Vanilla access:** no item gate is documented.
- **Boss requirement:** any weapon; a single bomb tends to drop a bomb pack.
- **Hint phrases:** Manhandla Threatens.

## Remember

The Raft found here opens the island of Level 4. Z1R never places the Raft on an island that normally needs it, so it cannot be locked behind its own use.

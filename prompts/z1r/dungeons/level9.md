# Level 9, Death Mountain (`level9`)

- **Shape:** Death Mountain, the final level, also called the Skull.
- **Boss:** Ganon (spelled Gannon on the Z1R wiki), invisible until a sword hit turns him red; a Silver Arrow ends him. Patra, an eye boss with orbiting eyes, holds the map.
- **Vanilla items:** Silver Arrows and the Red Ring. There is no Heart Container or Triforce piece here.
- **Vanilla access:** eight Triforce pieces; Spectacle Rock bombed; the Ladder to cross the river.
- **Boss requirement:** the Bow and Silver Arrows plus a sword; swordless mode uses the Wand. Patra falls to the sword or the Wand.
- **Hint phrases:** "entrance to death". The lines SPECTACLE ROCK IS AN ENTRANCE TO DEATH, PATRA HAS THE MAP and EYES OF SKULL HAS A SECRET are Level 9 hints, and "Enemy" Guards The Compass names the compass room.

## Entry and Gannon facts

- An old man blocks entry without 8 Triforce pieces, and the lobby cannot be bombed out of, so the requirement cannot be skipped. The Level 9 entry setting can be 0 to 8 pieces, open, a random count, specific pieces or an item.
- Level 9 holds Gannon, Zelda, two staircase items, four hints and normally six transport stairs. Wizzrobes, teleporting wizards that fire beams, are common inside.
- Gannon's neighbors show -ROAR- instead of -LIFE-. Zelda's room has a shutter that opens only after Gannon dies and cannot be bombed into.
- Force Gannon Fight makes Gannon mandatory before Zelda. The Gannon Kill Kit is the Bow and Silver Arrows together.
- The Raft, Ladder, Recorder, Bow and Bracelet are kept out of Level 9 unless Allow Important Items in 9 is on, and in swordless mode the Wand too.

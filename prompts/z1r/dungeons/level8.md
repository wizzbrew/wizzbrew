# Level 8, the Lion (`level8`)

- **Shape:** Lion.
- **Boss:** a four-headed Gleeok. In the second quest it is the boss of Level 7.
- **Vanilla items:** Magic Book and Magic Key, plus the Heart Container, Triforce piece, map and compass.
- **Vanilla access:** the Candle. The entrance is a tree that must be burned.
- **Boss requirement:** sword or Wand on the heads.
- **Hint phrases:** Gleeok Returns.

## Remember

The Magic Key opens every locked door and is never used up. The Book makes the Wand shoot fire. In the second quest the Magic Key room needs the Ladder, so the Ladder is never placed past it.

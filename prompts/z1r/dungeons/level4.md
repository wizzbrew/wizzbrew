# Level 4, the Snake (`level4`)

- **Shape:** Snake.
- **Boss:** Gleeok, a multi-headed dragon whose heads detach and fly at you.
- **Vanilla items:** Stepladder, plus the Heart Container, Triforce piece, map and compass.
- **Vanilla access:** the Raft. The level sits on an island.
- **Boss requirement:** sword or Wand on the heads.
- **Hint phrases:** Gleeok Lurks. The hint Sail Across the Water means the Raft is needed.

## Remember

The Stepladder found here is the item that crosses one-tile water gaps. Z1R never places the Raft on an island that needs the Raft, which covers the original Level 4 island and the island heart container. With dungeon-only cave shuffle any level can sit under a lake or behind the Raft.

# Level 6, the Dragon (`level6`)

- **Shape:** Dragon.
- **Boss:** Gohma, a crab that is hurt only by arrows into its open eye.
- **Vanilla items:** Magic Wand, plus the Heart Container, Triforce piece, map and compass.
- **Vanilla access:** no item gate is documented.
- **Boss requirement:** the Bow and arrows into the open eye.
- **Hint phrases:** Gohma Creeps. The line AIM AT THE EYES OF GOHMA says arrows, and Fire the Arrow to Obtain [item] means the Bow is needed because Gohma blocks the path.

## Remember

Z1R never makes the path to the Bow include a room where Gohma must be killed. Swordless mode never makes the path to the Wand include a room with a Gleeok or Pols Voice that must be killed.

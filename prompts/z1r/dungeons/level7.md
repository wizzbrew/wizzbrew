# Level 7, the Demon (`level7`)

- **Shape:** Demon.
- **Boss:** Aquamentus again. A Hungry Goriya guards a door inside and wants Bait.
- **Vanilla items:** Red Candle, plus the Heart Container, Triforce piece, map and compass. A Bomb Upgrade Man inside sells more bomb capacity for 100 rupees.
- **Vanilla access:** the Recorder, played at the pond where no fairy lives. Z1R states that the first quest lakes at Levels 2 and 7 always open with the Recorder.
- **Boss requirement:** any weapon for Aquamentus; Bait for the Hungry Goriya.
- **Hint phrases:** Goriya Grumbles. The lines GRUMBLE, GRUMBLE... and THERE ARE SECRETS WHERE FAIRIES DON'T LIVE belong here.

## Remember

Bait is bought in a shop; one version of the shop sells it for 60 rupees. Z1R keeps at least one Bait shop reachable without the Raft, Recorder, Bracelet or Ladder. Rooms in Level 7 need the Ladder in both quests, so the Ladder is never placed beyond them.

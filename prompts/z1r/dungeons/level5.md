# Level 5, the Lizard (`level5`)

- **Shape:** Lizard.
- **Boss:** Digdogger, a giant eye that the Recorder splits into small ones.
- **Vanilla items:** Recorder, plus the Heart Container, Triforce piece, map and compass.
- **Vanilla access:** no item gate is documented.
- **Boss requirement:** the Recorder to split it, then a reusable weapon.
- **Hint phrases:** Digdogger Gazes. The line DIGDOGGER HATES CERTAIN KIND OF SOUND points at the Recorder, and Play a Melody to Obtain [item] can mean a Digdogger blocks an item.

## Remember

The Recorder is also the item that reveals lakes and warps to dungeons. Z1R never makes the path to the Recorder include a room where Digdogger must be killed, and never places the Recorder under the lake or anywhere it opens.

# Items, hearts, sword upgrades and the item pool

## The item list

Each row gives what the item does, where it sits in the vanilla first quest, and what it opens in logic. Z1R can move any of these.

| Item | Effect and vanilla spot | What it gates in logic |
|---|---|---|
| Wood Sword | The first sword, given by the old man in the first cave ("IT'S DANGEROUS TO GO ALONE! TAKE THIS.") | The Swords setting can start without it (No Wood Sword puts the White Sword in logic) or keep it as the only sword |
| White Sword | The second sword, above the northern waterfall; needs hearts, 5 in vanilla | The cave can need the Recorder or a Feat of Strength (hints tell which). A flag can replace it with another item |
| Magical Sword | The third sword, under a gravestone in the Graveyard; needs 12 hearts in vanilla | Heart count and the gravestone |
| Boomerang | Stuns enemies and fetches items; vanilla Level 1 | No gate is documented |
| Magical Boomerang | Flies farther; vanilla Level 2 | No gate is documented |
| Bow | Shoots arrows; vanilla Level 1 | Gohma (arrows into its eye), item stairs that Gohma blocks, and Ganon. Hint: Fire the Arrow |
| Arrows | Cost one rupee each | Needed with the Bow; an Arrow shop is guaranteed reachable without Raft, Recorder, Bracelet or Ladder |
| Silver Arrows | The finisher for Ganon; vanilla Level 9 | Needed to finish Ganon. Beating the game takes a sword, the Bow, the Silver Arrows and the Triforces |
| Bombs | Open walls and kill Dodongos; Bomb Upgrade Men sell more capacity (100 rupees in Level 7) | Secret walls; Dodongo |
| Blue Candle | Burns bushes and lights dark rooms, once per screen; bought in a shop | Burnable bushes. A Blue Candle shop is guaranteed reachable |
| Red Candle | Burns bushes and lights dark rooms repeatedly; vanilla Level 7 | The Level 8 entrance (a tree to burn) |
| Blue Ring | Enemies hit for half damage | No gate is documented |
| Red Ring | Enemies hit for a quarter damage; vanilla Level 9 | No gate is documented |
| Power Bracelet | Lets Link push heavy rocks and gravestones; vanilla spot is under an Armos | Rocks and gravestones that hide caves, some levels and the White Sword cave. Hint: A Feat of Strength. Kept out of Level 9 |
| Raft | Crosses water at docks; vanilla Level 3 | The island of Level 4 and the island heart container. Hint: Sail Across the Water. Never placed on an island that needs it; kept out of Level 9 |
| Stepladder (Ladder) | Crosses one-tile water gaps; vanilla Level 4 | The river to Death Mountain and Level 9, the Coast heart and moat rooms in dungeons. Hint: Step Over the Water. Screen scrolling is the usual workaround. Kept out of Level 9 |
| Recorder (whistle) | Reveals hidden dungeons at lakes, stuns Digdogger and warps to cleared dungeons; vanilla Level 5 | Level 7, Digdogger and some caves. Hint: Play a Melody. Never placed under the lake or anywhere it opens; kept out of Level 9 |
| Magic Wand | Shoots beams; vanilla Level 6 | A sword alternative against Gleeok and Patra, and the weapon against Ganon in swordless mode. With the Book it shoots fire |
| Magic Book | Makes the Wand's beams fire; vanilla Level 8 | No gate is documented; it works through the Wand |
| Magic Key | Opens every locked door and is never used up; vanilla Level 8 | Locked doors |
| Bait | Feeds the Hungry Goriya; bought in a shop | The Goriya door in Level 7 and any Bait Blocks (one can sit below Zelda's room). A Bait shop is guaranteed reachable |
| Letter | Lets Link buy medicine from the old woman; in a cave in the northeast | The potion shop |
| Magic Shield | Blocks fireballs; Like Likes can eat it; bought in a shop | No gate is documented |
| Heart Container | Raises maximum health; one in each of Levels 1 to 8 | Sword hearts. Item shuffle can place them anywhere |
| Triforce piece | A fragment of the Triforce of Wisdom; one in each of Levels 1 to 8 | Level 9 entry (8 in vanilla) |

Each level also holds a map and a compass. Players call a Triforce piece a Dorito, tringle or chip.

## Hearts and sword upgrades

- In vanilla the White Sword needs 5 hearts and the Magical Sword needs 12. Z1R flags can set the White Sword to 4 to 6 and the Magical Sword to 10 to 14. The Rookie Rumble 2026 flagset uses 10 for the Magical Sword.
- Starting hearts can be 1 to 16, but this does not generate additional Heart Containers, so starting low does not lower the cap.
- One-hit KO does not remove the heart requirement for the White Sword item.
- Item shuffle can place Heart Containers anywhere (Items + Hearts) or keep one per dungeon, and the Coast and White Sword spots can hold hearts or other items.
- Mugger rooms ("Leave your life or money") can cost a heart container, bomb capacity, keys or rupees.
- Swords setting: Normal; Wood Sword Only; No Wood Sword (the White Sword is in logic); Swordless (bombs replace the White and Magical Swords and Ganon needs the Wand); Random.

## What kills what

| Enemy | Item it asks for |
|---|---|
| Dodongo | Bombs, which it eats |
| Digdogger | The Recorder splits it into small ones |
| Gohma | Arrows into its open eye |
| Hungry Goriya | Bait |
| Pols Voice | Sword, Wand or Bow |
| Ganon | The Bow and Silver Arrows, plus a sword (the Wand if swordless) |
| Like Like | Eats the shield |
| Bubble | Disables the sword for a while |

Other dungeon enemies: Moldorm is a segmented worm, Lanmola a tail-thrashing sand worm nicknamed the danger noodle, and Wizzrobes teleport and fire beams, most of all in Level 9. Darknuts, Goriyas, Stalfos, Keese, Wallmasters, Gibdos, Zols, Gels and Vires are the common fodder.

## The item pool and start items

- Item shuffle options: None; Dungeon Items (a heart on each dungeon floor); Items Anywhere with Hearts within Dungeons; Items + Hearts; Random.
- Shuffle dungeon drops puts Triforce pieces, hearts, keys, the compass and the map in the shuffle; off, the Triforce pieces sit in the Triforce room. Shuffle minor dungeon drops adds bombs, five-rupee and key drops, and one floor can then hold several major items.
- The White Sword, Armos, Coast and Items in 9 spots can each hold a fixed item or a random one from the pool: Book, Boomerang, Bow, Heart Container, Ladder, Magical Boomerang, Magical Key, Power Bracelet, Raft, Recorder, Red Candle, Red Ring, Silver Arrow, Wand and White Sword. The Coast spot excludes the Ladder, which is needed to reach it.
- Shop shuffle covers shop items and prices, secret rupee values (10, 30 and 100 in vanilla), door repair, the Blue Candle in extra caves, bomb upgrade cost and size, Money Making Game prizes and the Armos position.
- Start items can be the Raft, Bow, 8 Bombs, Recorder, Bait, Magical Key, Letter, Bracelet, Ladder, Wand, Book, swords, arrows, candles, rings and boomerangs, with 1 to 16 starting hearts and 0 to 8 Triforces.

# Level 8 (`level8`): logic rules

## Entry rule

The Candle, to burn the tree at the entrance. The Blue Candle burns once per screen and the Red Candle repeatedly; the Red Candle is the vanilla item from Level 7.

## Inside the level

The four-headed Gleeok needs the sword or the Wand on its heads. The vanilla items are the Magic Book, which makes the Wand shoot fire, and the Magic Key, which opens every locked door and is never used up.

## Guarantees that mention this level

Ladder path: in unshuffled rooms the guarantee covers the Magic Key room in Level 8 in the second quest, so the Ladder is never placed past it. At least one Blue Candle shop is reachable from overworld items, not under a bush, on an island, or behind the Recorder or Ladder.

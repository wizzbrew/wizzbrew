# Level 3 (`level3`): logic rules

## Entry rule

No item gate is documented for the vanilla entrance. With cave shuffle it can sit behind any gate.

## Inside the level

Manhandla needs any weapon. The vanilla item is the Raft, which the logic needs for the island of Level 4 and the island heart container. A single bomb tends to drop a bomb pack in a Manhandla room.

## Guarantees that mention this level

The Raft is never placed on an island that normally needs it. The Raft, Ladder, Recorder, Bow and Bracelet are kept out of Level 9 unless Allow Important Items in 9 is on, and the FAQ promises all of them can be had before Level 9.

# Level 2 (`level2`): logic rules

## Entry rule

No item gate is documented for the vanilla entrance. Z1R states that the first quest lakes at Levels 2 and 7 always open with the Recorder. With cave shuffle or a Mixed Overworld the entrance can move, and the wiki's own example of dungeon-only cave shuffle has Level 2 needing the Raft and Level 4 needing the Recorder.

## Inside the level

Dodongo is killed by bombs, which it eats; a sword also works with enough hits. The vanilla item is the Magical Boomerang, which has no documented gate.

## Guarantees that mention this level

None names Level 2 directly. The guarantee that the Raft is never placed on an island that normally needs the Raft, and the one that keeps the Recorder out of any location it opens, are the ones that matter when a shuffle puts this level behind a lake or a dock.

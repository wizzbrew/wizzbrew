# Level 1 (`level1`): logic rules

## Entry rule

None. Level 1 has no item gate in vanilla, so it is reachable from the start. With cave shuffle its entrance can sit behind any gate, and the Overworld Quest and Dungeon Quest settings can move it.

## Inside the level

Aquamentus needs a weapon: the sword or its beam on the horn. The vanilla items here are the Bow and the Boomerang; with item shuffle on, either can sit in any other level and any item can sit here.

## Guarantees that mention this level

None. No Z1R guarantee names Level 1; the guarantees cover the Bow, Recorder, Wand, Ladder, Raft, shops and Level 9.

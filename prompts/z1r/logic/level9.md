# Level 9 (`level9`): logic rules

## Entry rule

Eight Triforce pieces in vanilla; an old man blocks entry otherwise, and the lobby cannot be bombed out of. The Spectacle Rock must be bombed and the Ladder crosses the river. The Level 9 entry setting can be 0 to 8 pieces, open, a random count, specific pieces or an item.

## Inside the level

Ganon needs the Bow and Silver Arrows plus a sword; swordless mode needs the Wand instead of a sword. Patra needs the sword or the Wand. Force Gannon Fight makes the Gannon fight mandatory before Zelda.

## Guarantees that mention this level

Level 9 item restrictions: the Raft, Ladder, Recorder, Bow and Power Bracelet are kept out of Level 9 unless Allow Important Items in 9 is on, and in swordless mode the Wand is kept out too. The FAQ promises that before Level 9 you can get the Ladder, Recorder, Bow, Power Bracelet and Raft, and that Silver Arrows may be needed for Ganon; this can fail if Level 9 is unlocked and important items are allowed in 9.

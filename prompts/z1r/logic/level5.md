# Level 5 (`level5`): logic rules

## Entry rule

No item gate is documented for the vanilla entrance. With cave shuffle it can sit behind any gate.

## Inside the level

Digdogger needs the Recorder to split it, then a reusable weapon. The vanilla item is the Recorder, which reveals lakes, warps to cleared dungeons and stuns Digdogger. The hint Play a Melody to Obtain [item] can mean a Digdogger blocks an item.

## Guarantees that mention this level

The path to the Recorder never includes a room where Digdogger must be killed. The Recorder is never placed under the lake or in any location the Recorder opens, and it stays out of Level 9 unless Allow Important Items in 9 is on.

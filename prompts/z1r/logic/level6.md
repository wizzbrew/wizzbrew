# Level 6 (`level6`): logic rules

## Entry rule

No item gate is documented for the vanilla entrance. With cave shuffle it can sit behind any gate.

## Inside the level

Gohma needs the Bow and arrows into its open eye. The hint Fire the Arrow to Obtain [item] means the Bow is needed because Gohma blocks the path. The vanilla item is the Magic Wand, which is a sword alternative and, with the Book, shoots fire.

## Guarantees that mention this level

The path to the Bow never includes a room where Gohma must be killed. In swordless mode the path to the Wand never includes a room with a Gleeok or Pols Voice that must be killed, and the Wand stays out of Level 9.

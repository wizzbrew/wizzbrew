# Level 4 (`level4`): logic rules

## Entry rule

The Raft, because the level sits on an island reached from a dock. The hint Sail Across the Water means the Raft is needed. With dungeon-only cave shuffle any level can sit under a lake or behind the Raft.

## Inside the level

Gleeok needs the sword or the Wand on its heads. The vanilla item is the Stepladder, which gates one-tile water gaps: the river to Death Mountain and Level 9, the Coast heart and moat rooms in dungeons.

## Guarantees that mention this level

Raft guarantee: the Raft is never placed on an island that normally needs the Raft, which covers the original Level 4 and the island heart container. Ladder guarantee: the path to the Ladder never includes a room that needs the Ladder.

# Level 7 (`level7`): logic rules

## Entry rule

The Recorder, played at the pond where no fairy lives (THERE ARE SECRETS WHERE FAIRIES DON'T LIVE). Z1R states that the first quest lakes at Levels 2 and 7 always open with the Recorder.

## Inside the level

Aquamentus needs any weapon. The Hungry Goriya needs Bait, which is bought in a shop. Rooms in Level 7 need the Ladder in both quests. The vanilla item is the Red Candle, which burns the tree at the Level 8 entrance.

## Guarantees that mention this level

Ladder path: the path to the Ladder never includes a room that needs the Ladder, and in unshuffled rooms that covers Level 7 in both quests. At least one Bait shop is reachable without the Raft, Recorder, Bracelet or Ladder.
